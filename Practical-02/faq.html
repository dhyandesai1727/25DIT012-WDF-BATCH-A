Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find quick answers regarding registration, event participation, and portal access.

Q1: Who can register on StudentHub?

Any enrolled student of CHARUSAT Faculty of Technology & Engineering (FTE) across IT, CSE, and CE departments can register using their university email.

Q2: Is there any registration fee for campus technical events?

Most department-level workshops and competitions are completely free for students. Certain national-level hackathons may have nominal team entry fees mentioned on the event poster.

Q3: How do I recover my password if I forget it?

You can visit the IT departmental lab coordinator or submit a request through the Contact Us page for account reset.

Q4: Can I participate in multiple events during TechFest?

Yes, provided the event timings and schedules do not conflict with each other.
