About StudentHub & Faculty of Technology

StudentHub was developed as part of the Web Development Frameworks laboratory curriculum to foster practical engineering skills in full-stack architecture, semantic HTML5, and responsive design.

Our Vision & Mission

To cultivate future-ready IT engineers who build inclusive, accessible, and high-performance digital platforms for society.

Mission

Provide students with hands-on exposure to practical industry frameworks, accessibility standards, and secure backend engineering principles.
